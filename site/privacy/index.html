Privacy

Your manifests and transcripts stay on your device.

What the checker processes

The web app, browser extension, and CLI read files or text that you deliberately provide. Analysis happens locally in your browser or Node process. The product does not send or store that content.

Browser access

The extension requests no host permissions and does not read tabs, page content, cookies, credentials, browsing history, or network traffic. It does not execute MCP tools.

Storage and analytics

The inspector does not save reports or inputs to browser storage. Export happens only when you request a local download. The site has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts. Its service worker caches public app files for offline use, never your input.

Contact and changes

This policy is effective 28 August 2026. Material changes will be published here. Questions can be opened in the project’s public issue tracker.
